I am currently pursing my Post Graduate studies in Urban Spatial Science from University College London (UCL), UK. Here are some of the projects i've worked on over the years.

Assessing the Impact of Rural Road Infrastructure on Development in India using Satellite Derived Night Time Light as Proxy"

The study employs econometric methods such as PSM, DID, DiDiD and RDD to assess the impact of India's National Road Program on development as indicated by changes in nighttime light intensity. The findings revealed positive long term impact of program, particularly during later post-treatment years.

Impact of Built Environment Factors on House Prices in London Boroughs"

The study explores the relationship between different elements of built environment and house prices in London boroughs.The analysis reveals that commercial areas have a statistically significant and positive impact, while other elements did not show significant effects once multicollinearity was accounted for.

Strategic Route Planning and Economic Analysis for Delivery Services of a City"

The primary goal of this NetLogo model was to evaluate financial performance of a delivery company operating within a city using real-world geographic-data to optimize operational strategies. The simulation not only provides insights into no. of deliveries but also enables to refine financial performance.

CROPINVEST - Crop Yield Estimator

Group Project: Created a GEE application that monitors the environment to predict crop yields, which in turn can help end users (banks, crop insurance companies ,etc) to accurately and scientifically capture the farms potential to make informed decisions.

Investigating Survival Disparity : Comparative Analysis of Cities with Unequal Resources.

This study aimed to examine the influence of unequal resource distribution, represented by sugar patches, on the final surviving population in the two neighboring cities with similar demographics. Models Used - "Immediate Growback Model" and "Constant Growback Model".

Spatial Clustering of Public Service Facilities in Andhra Pradesh, India

This study explores the spatial clustering of the different public service facilities for one of the newest split states of India viz. Andhra Pradesh. The reorganization led to a redistribution of resources and our study explored the distribution & the spatial clustering of facilities thus exploring the regional disparities.

Implementation of Machine Learning Techniques for detecting temporal change in LULC in a city

I explored the used of Classification and Regression Trees (CART) and Random forests (RF) using Google Earth Engine for Srinagar City. For, this exercise I identified three types of landcovers - water, urban and forest.

Comparing Socio-spatial Traits in Two London Neighbourhoods using Soja’s Socio-spatial Dialectic

In this study, I compared and described the sociospatial characteristics of two neighbourhoods in the London borough of Tower Hamlets: Whitechapel and Canary Wharf and further explore role of socio-spatial dialectic in understanding their very different outcomes.
